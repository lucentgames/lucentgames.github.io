Dotrix

Delete your account

Dotrix by Lucent Games. You can delete your account and everything saved with it at any time.

In the game

  1. Open Dotrix and go to Profile.
  2. Tap the gear to open Settings.
  3. Scroll to the bottom and tap Delete all my data.
  4. Confirm. You may be asked to sign in to Google Play Games or Game Center again first.

This deletes your account, its cloud save, and your online profile, friend code and friends straight away, along with everything on your phone.

Without the game

If you no longer have the game, email dev.lucent.app@gmail.com with the subject "Delete my Dotrix account", and tell us your player name and friend code (in Profile > Friends) if you know them. We'll delete it within 30 days and reply to let you know.

What's deleted

  • Your sign-in with Dotrix.
  • Your cloud save: progress, stars, Sparks, Gems and unlocked items.

What's kept

  • Records of purchases, which Google Play, the App Store and RevenueCat keep for as long as the law requires (for tax and refunds).

Deleting your data doesn't refund purchases. If you play again later, Restore purchases in Settings brings back Remove Ads and the starter pack's frame.
