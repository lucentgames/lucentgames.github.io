Dotrix

Privacy policy

Last updated 8 November 2026

Dotrix is a puzzle game made by Lucent Games ("we", "us"). This policy explains what information the game uses, why, and the choices you have. If you have any questions, email us at dev.lucent.app@gmail.com.

The short version

  • Your progress is saved on your phone. If you sign in, it's also kept in the cloud so you don't lose it.
  • We show ads to keep Dotrix free. In the UK and EU, ads are only personalised if you agree. On iPhone, they're only personalised if you allow tracking.
  • Purchases are handled by Google Play or the App Store. We never see your card details.
  • We don't sell your personal information.

What we use, and why

Your game

Your stars, times, streaks, unlocked items, Sparks, Gems and settings are saved on your device. If you choose to sign in with Apple or Google, we also keep a copy in the cloud, linked to a sign-in identifier, so your progress follows you to a new phone. This copy is stored with Google Firebase. We use this information to run the game you asked for.

Ads

Dotrix shows ads from Google AdMob: optional videos you choose to watch for rewards, and occasional ads between puzzles. To show and measure ads, Google may collect your device's advertising identifier, IP address, and basic device and app information.

  • UK and EU: we ask for your consent using Google's consent form before ads are personalised. You can change your choice at any time in Settings → Privacy and ad choices. If you don't consent, you'll still see ads, but they won't be based on your interests.
  • iPhone: Dotrix asks whether it may use your advertising identifier. If you say no, ads aren't personalised by tracking. You see the same number of ads either way.

Buying Remove Ads, or any premium season pass, turns off ads between puzzles for good. Google's privacy policy explains how Google uses this information: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

Purchases

Purchases are made through Google Play or the Apple App Store, which handle payment. We use RevenueCat to confirm purchases and restore them on a new device. RevenueCat receives a random app user identifier and the details of what was bought and when, but never your payment details. See revenuecat.com/privacy.

Keeping the game working

We use Google Firebase to run sign-in, cloud saves and settings we can change without an app update. Firebase may process your IP address and device information to do this. See firebase.google.com/support/privacy.

Legal bases (UK and EU)

We use your game data and purchase records to provide the game you asked for (contract). We use your data for ads based on our legitimate interest in keeping the game free, and we personalise ads only with your consent.

Sharing

We share information only with the services named above, which process it for us, or when the law requires it. Some of these services store data outside the UK and EU, such as in the United States. They do so under safeguards such as the UK and EU standard contractual clauses.

How long we keep it

Information on your device stays until you delete the app or use Settings → Delete all my data. Cloud saves stay until you delete your account, and we then remove them within 30 days. Purchase records are kept as long as the law requires.

Your rights

You can ask to see, correct, move or delete your information, and you can object to how it's used. Email dev.lucent.app@gmail.com. You also have the right to complain to the Information Commissioner's Office (ico.org.uk) or your local data protection authority.

Children

Dotrix isn't aimed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect information from them. If you believe a child has given us information, email us and we'll delete it.

Changes

If we change this policy, we'll update the date at the top. Big changes will also be shown in the game.
